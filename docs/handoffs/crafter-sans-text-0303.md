## Drawing change

Text 0.303 opens the inner curves where shoulders/bowls meet stems in
`n/h/m/u` and `b/d/p/q`. The accents are preserved and recomposed on revised
n/u bases. Eighteen glyphs change in each of the four weights:
`b d h m n ń ň ñ p q u ú û ü ù ū ů ũ`.

Only interior Bézier controls change. Exterior silhouettes, straight stems,
counter extrema, glyph bounds, advances, kerning, vertical metrics and the
0.301 calibrated scale are retained. The local geometry comparison finds
no added ink; affected glyph areas decrease approximately 0.7–2%.
All three Display 0.200 binaries and approved `c/r/a/f/t/e/g/&` seeds remain.
The i/I accent correction from 0.302 remains.

The generator records the drawing decisions and checks the expected source
curves before replacing them. Generated SVG/UFO files are still outputs,
not separate manual masters. The family remains four experimental static
cuts; hinting, compatible variable masters and italics are not completed.
