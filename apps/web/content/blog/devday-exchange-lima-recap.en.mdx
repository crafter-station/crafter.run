On October 21, 2026, we hosted DevDay Exchange Community: Lima at the auditorium of the Faculty of Systems Engineering and Computer Science (FISI) at UNMSM in Lima. The event was free and held in Spanish, and it was part of the [Codex Community Events](https://luma.com/1iz8daqt) calendar, where 86 people were approved. Most of them were students.

## A DevDay recap in four parts

<Figure src="/blog/devday-exchange-lima-recap/podium.webp" alt="Railly Hugo at the podium in front of the projected event title slide" caption="Railly opening the night." size="1440x960" wide />

OpenAI shipped about twenty launches in one day at its keynote. We did not cover them all. We picked the ones that change how we build and split the deck between two people from the team.

- **Railly Hugo** opened with the Codex Originals video and explained what Crafter Station is: the community, our open source projects and Makeables.
- **Edward Ramos** covered always-on agents: Dots, ChatGPT Space, AppShots and what is new for builders in ChatGPT, such as plugin extensions and MCP events.
- **Models and APIs** we split between the two of us: GPT-6.1 Sol, Ultrafast, the Decisions API, the Agents API and GPT Live.
- **Railly** closed with Codex: Codex Cloud, the new CLI and code review.

## Codex built an app live

For the demo, we asked Codex, from the terminal and in Spanish, for an app for DevDay at San Marcos. While we explained what it was doing, Codex wrote a wheel to raffle prizes among attendees.

Every attendee also got an OpenAI API credit code to keep building at home.

## What we will change next time

We planned a block for every attendee to build something with Codex and show it in two minutes. We did not get to it: the opening took longer than expected, and the room did not have outlets for that many laptops. For the next event:

- A short opening, with one person in charge of the schedule.
- A timed build & ship session, in a venue with outlets and stable internet.
- Check-in at the door, so we know how many people actually came.
- A format designed for students: more guided and hands-on.
