Hot Reload is a coffee meetup for the dev community. The first edition was on Saturday, October 17, 2026, at Don Salazar Specialty Coffee in Miraflores, Lima. It happened thanks to [Vercel](https://vercel.com), who covered breakfast. It was held in Spanish. We opened 20 spots and about 15 people came.

## Coffee and side projects

The idea was simple. Everyone brought a side project to show or a question for the table, and we talked about what we were building.

## How the Research crew found us

At that table we met [Diego Jesus](/en/team/crew) and [Antony Alvarez](/en/team/crew), political scientists from [DATAdaf](https://datadaf.com). They are now part of the Crafter Research crew, where they work with electoral and social science data.

For us, that is the best part of a small event: you leave with a conversation that keeps going.

## Cristian Olivera, from the table to the core team

At the same table was [Cristian Olivera](/en/team/cristianolivera1), creator of [Openvid](https://openvid.dev), the video editor for devs that took first place at the [CubePath 2026 hackathon with midudev](https://cubepath.com/es/hackatones). That morning we personally invited him to join the Crafter Station core team.

In less than a month he built [Muelle](https://github.com/crafter-station/Muelle), a macOS-style dock for Windows 11. It passed 250 GitHub stars in its first week.

## What we're trying in 02

- A "bring your project" format: short pitches and one question for everyone, "how can we help?", so partnerships can come out of it.
- Physical stickers on the table to break the ice from the start.
- A required phone number on Luma and a form that asks what you are building.
- Guests and check-in on [Makeables](https://makeables.dev) events, our own tool.
- About 30 people including the team, and a photographer.
