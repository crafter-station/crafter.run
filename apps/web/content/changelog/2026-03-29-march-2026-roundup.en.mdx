March 2026 had 3 smaller ships, all from Station. Two bring consumer services to the terminal. spoti-cli searches, recommends and builds Spotify playlists, and rappi-cli, the most starred ship of the month, orders from Rappi through a terminal, a REST API or an MCP server. The third, Codex Globe, maps OpenAI Codex Ambassadors on an interactive 3D globe.
