Badge Studio is a design studio for event badges with personality. Pick an art direction, put your photo and name across the whole collection, edit every layer, change the material and export it. Your coding agent can design one with you through the `badgio` CLI. Try it at [badge-studio.crafter.run](https://badge-studio.crafter.run).

## One photo, every style

Start with your name and a photo, or a sample one, and you see yourself in every direction at once: hackathon badges, physical originals like Térmico, Prisma and Cromo, and community designs. Your photo stays in your browser, and you can explore without an account.

## Edit every layer

The editor shows the badge as layers you can select, hide or lock, with panels for the background and material, the participant details, front and back faces, and PNG or JSON export.

<Figure src="/changelog/badge-studio/editor.webp" alt="Badge Studio editor with a layered GTM Hackathon badge, its layer list and export controls" size="1280x800" caption="A layered design with 45 layers, ready to flip and export." />

## Design with your agent

The `badgio` CLI lists the styles, creates and validates badge documents, and opens a live preview your agent can edit through the same tools as the editor. Publishing to the public gallery needs an account and asks before it runs.

## Try it

Open [badge-studio.crafter.run](https://badge-studio.crafter.run), or start from your terminal. The CLI needs Node.js 22 or newer:

```bash
npx badgio styles list
npx badgio design create --style gtm --out badge.json
npx badgio design validate --file badge.json
```

Import the JSON in the editor to keep going visually.
