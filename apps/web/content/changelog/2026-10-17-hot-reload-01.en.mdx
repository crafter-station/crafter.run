Hot Reload is a coffee with the dev community. Edition 01 took place on October 17, 2026 at Don Salazar Specialty Coffee in Miraflores, Lima, with 20 seats and Vercel as partner. People brought a side project to show or a question for the table.

On the site, each guest gets a collectible 3D badge with lanyard variants and an attendance stamp, and the page hides a game, Espresso perfecto, built with dotframe.

See the edition at [crafter.run/events/hot-reload](https://crafter.run/en/events/hot-reload).
