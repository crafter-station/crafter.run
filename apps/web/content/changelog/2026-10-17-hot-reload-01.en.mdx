Hot Reload is a coffee with the dev community. Edition 01 took place on October 17, 2026 at Don Salazar Specialty Coffee in Miraflores, Lima, with 20 seats and Vercel as partner. People brought a side project to show or a question for the table. See the edition at [crafter.run/events/hot-reload](https://crafter.run/en/events/hot-reload).

## A badge and a game

Each guest gets a collectible 3D badge with lanyard variants and an attendance stamp, after signing in with the email they used on Luma. The page also hides a game: in Espresso perfecto you hold to pour and release on the line, and three ruined cups close the café.

## See it

Open [crafter.run/events/hot-reload](https://crafter.run/en/events/hot-reload) and press G to play, or see the edition on [Luma](https://luma.com/7o3puw27).
