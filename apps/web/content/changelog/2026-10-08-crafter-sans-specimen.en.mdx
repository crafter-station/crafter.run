The Crafter Sans specimen is public at [crafter.run/font](/en/font). Crafter Sans is the typeface we draw for Crafter Station, and the specimen shows how each letter is built, in two optical sizes, on a WebGPU background.

<Clip src="/changelog/crafter-sans-specimen/display-text.mp4" poster="/changelog/crafter-sans-specimen/display-text.webp" size="1280x800" alt="The specimen hero switching between Crafter Sans Display and Text" caption="Hover the letters to see their outlines. Switch between Display and Text at the same size." />

## One letter, two sizes

Crafter Sans comes in Display, for headlines, and Text, for reading. The hero draws both from the real font outlines, so you can compare them at the same size and see where the curves change.

## Try it with your own words

Type anything, then change the variant, size and spacing to see how it holds up.

<Clip src="/changelog/crafter-sans-specimen/try-it.mp4" poster="/changelog/crafter-sans-specimen/try-it.webp" size="1280x800" alt="Typing a phrase in the specimen tester and switching to Text Bold" caption="The tester uses the same fonts the site ships." />

## Every curve, up close

Pick any character to see its actual outline, with advance, x-height and cap height marked. The page also shows Crafter Sans in context, on the brand pieces it was drawn for.
