hack0 gives visibility to tech communities and events in Latin America. It reads events from Luma calendars, Meetup groups and Eventbrite organizers, merges the same event seen in several places, and publishes the result as a single map and calendar.

You can subscribe to the calendar or browse the full archive at [hack0.dev](https://hack0.dev).
