The Next Craft is Crafter Station's in-person hackathon. On August 29, 2026, teams of three to five worked for 12 hours at the same time in Lima, Bogota, Guatemala, Arequipa and El Salvador, going from zero to a product someone real could use. The site keeps the tracks, schedule and rubric at [thenextcraft.org](https://thenextcraft.org).

## Three tracks

Content Machine was for tools that create, edit, publish or distribute content. Out of the Box was for weird and experimental builds with a technical spark: real time, new interfaces, generative work or hardware. Learning by Shipping was for tools that teach by making you do, with real feedback. Each track came with starting points, and the bar was the same for all three: someone real has to use what you build before the bell rings.

<Figure src="/changelog/the-next-craft/tracks.webp" alt="The Learning by Shipping track card with its starting points" size="1280x800" caption="Each track lists starting points, not fixed problems." />

## One day, one rubric

The day ran from 09:00 to 21:00, with a code freeze and then live demos of three minutes per team. One rubric applied to every track and city: a closed demo that works end to end, validated use with a specific user and data collected that day, technical craft, ambition and a clear pitch. Local communities organized each city.
