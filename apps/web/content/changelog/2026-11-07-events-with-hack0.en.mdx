The events page on crafter.run now reads the public hack0 calendar, so it shows what is coming up across Latin America in one place. It is a small Crafter Station and hack0 fusion. See it at [crafter.run/events](https://crafter.run/en/events).

<Figure src="/changelog/events-with-hack0/calendar.webp" alt="crafter.run events page with the Crafter Station and hack0 header, the next event and a skyline of cities with event counts" size="1280x800" caption="The next event on the calendar, and how many events each city has coming up." />

## Search the whole archive

Switch between upcoming events and the full archive, search by event, place or organizer, and pick the time zone you want the times in.

<Clip src="/changelog/events-with-hack0/search.mp4" poster="/changelog/events-with-hack0/search.webp" size="1280x800" alt="Opening the events archive on crafter.run, searching for hackathon and clicking a result" caption="Search the archive, then open the event on hack0.dev." />

## Every event opens on hack0

Each event links to its page on hack0.dev, with the date, the place and the registration link. An event hack0 has not synced yet keeps its Luma link.

## Try it

Open [crafter.run/events](https://crafter.run/en/events), or subscribe to the calendar in Google, Apple or Outlook from the same page. To list an event, submit it on Luma from the Hosting something section.
