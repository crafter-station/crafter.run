peru-financia turns ONPE's political funding records into something a person can read. You can follow the money toward each party with a Sankey diagram per election, rank parties by amount received, and open the profile of any donor.

It matters because the raw ONPE tables are hard to browse. Everything is open source, so you can check the data and the code.

Try it at [peru-financia.crafter.ing](https://peru-financia.crafter.ing).
