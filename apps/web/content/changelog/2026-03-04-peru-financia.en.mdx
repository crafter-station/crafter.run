peru-financia turns ONPE's political funding records into something a person can read: who donates, how much, to which party and in which election. It is open source, built by Crafter Research on more than 13,250 ONPE records. Explore it at [peru-financia.crafter.ing](https://peru-financia.crafter.ing).

## Follow the money

Pick an election and the Sankey diagram shows where each party's money came from: individual donors, self-funding or unknown sources. The ranking orders parties by the amount they received, and the table lists every contribution, with search, a filter by type and a CSV download.

## Every donor has a profile

The donor index counts donors by type, shows how many fund two or more parties, and breaks donations down by size. Each donor opens to a profile with the total given, the parties funded and the full history.

<Figure src="/changelog/peru-financia/donors.webp" alt="peru-financia donor index with totals, top ten donors and distribution by amount" size="1280x800" caption="The donor index, from ONPE data." />

## Why it exists

The raw ONPE tables are hard to browse. peru-financia puts them in one place, and because the data and the code are public, anyone can check both.

## Try it

Open [peru-financia.crafter.ing](https://peru-financia.crafter.ing), choose an election, or search a party or donor by name from the top bar.
