Pulse 2.0 is live at [pulse.crafter.run](https://pulse.crafter.run). It is an internal dashboard of what Crafter Station ships across the crafter-station, crafter-research, crafter-lab and crafter-games organizations.

## What counts as a ship

A ship is a repository with a homepage or a published package. Pulse lists only those, so the dashboard shows work people can open and use.

## What is in it

Pulse 2.0 includes the team, the projects and the star history of each project.

## What changed

- The team leaderboard is built from the core roster and hides alumni and bots (#5).
- Pulse tracks crafter-lab and crafter-games, and syncs repository metadata every day (#6).
- The new interface adds ships, team, projects, traction and drill-down views (#7).
