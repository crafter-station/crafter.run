Pulse 2.0 is live at [pulse.crafter.run](https://pulse.crafter.run). It answers three questions about Crafter Station: what we shipped, who shipped it, and what is gaining traction. It covers all four organizations: crafter-station, crafter-research, crafter-lab and crafter-games.

<Clip src="/changelog/pulse-2/drill.mp4" poster="/changelog/pulse-2/drill.webp" size="1280x800" alt="Switching Pulse between week, quarter and month, then opening the list of projects shipped" caption="Every number opens the list behind it." />

## Ships, not commits

Pulse counts ships. A ship is a repository created in the period that has a homepage or a published package, so the headline number is work someone can open and use. Commits still show up per person, but they are not the score.

Switch between week, month and quarter, filter by organization, and click any number to see the repositories behind it.

## The team

The team page lists the ten people in the Crafter Station core with their ships, repositories and commits for the period. Bot accounts are excluded from every total.

<Figure src="/changelog/pulse-2/team.webp" alt="Pulse team page with ships, repos and commits per core member" size="1280x800" caption="Each card opens that person's ships and repositories." />

## Every project in one table

The projects page holds every repository across the four organizations, with search, filters by status and creator, and the stars each one gained in the period.

<Figure src="/changelog/pulse-2/projects.webp" alt="Pulse projects table with search, filters and stars gained" size="1280x800" caption="Stars gained are measured from daily snapshots." />

## What changed

- The leaderboard is built from the core roster, and bot accounts no longer inflate the totals ([#5](https://github.com/crafter-station/pulse/pull/5)).
- crafter-lab and crafter-games are tracked, and repository metadata syncs every day ([#6](https://github.com/crafter-station/pulse/pull/6)).
- The new interface adds ships, team, projects, star history and drill-down on every number ([#7](https://github.com/crafter-station/pulse/pull/7)).
