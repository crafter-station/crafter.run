December 2025 brought 5 smaller ships, all from Station. Two are about sharing with people you know, gift0 for wishlists and Crafter Chat for peer-to-peer messages that never touch the server. pintel turns drawing and guessing into a game where humans and models judge each other, pdf-summarizer works on the PDFs you upload, and the 2025 Wrapped page looked back on a year of open source building.

pintel has the most stars of the month, a game where people and models draw, guess and judge each other. Crafter Station 2025 Wrapped puts the year of open source building on one page at 2025.crafter.run. pdf-summarizer lets you upload a PDF, highlight text and create quicklinks to the parts you need.

## Everything that shipped

- **03 Dec**: [pintel](https://pintel.crafter.run): draw, guess and evaluate, a game where humans and models judge each other
- **10 Dec**: [gift0](https://gift0.lat): create and share wishlists with friends and family
- **21 Dec**: [pdf-summarizer](https://pdf-summarizer-eosin.vercel.app): upload PDFs, highlight text and create quicklinks
- **30 Dec**: [Crafter Station 2025 Wrapped](https://2025.crafter.run): a year of open source building, as a page
- **31 Dec**: [Crafter Chat](https://p2p-chat-mu.vercel.app): peer-to-peer messaging over WebRTC, where messages never touch the server

## By the numbers
