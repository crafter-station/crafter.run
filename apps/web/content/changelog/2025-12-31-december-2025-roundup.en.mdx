The smaller ships of the period, each with a live site, a published package or a release.

- [pintel](https://pintel.crafter.run): draw, guess and evaluate, a game where humans and models judge each other
- [gift0](https://gift0.lat): create and share wishlists with friends and family
- [pdf-summarizer](https://pdf-summarizer-eosin.vercel.app): upload PDFs, highlight text and create quicklinks
- [Crafter Station 2025 Wrapped](https://2025.crafter.run): a year of open source building, as a page
- [Crafter Chat](https://p2p-chat-mu.vercel.app): peer-to-peer messaging over WebRTC, where messages never touch the server
