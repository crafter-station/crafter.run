Sismo Abierto is an open-source layer over the public seismic data of Peru's Instituto Geofísico (IGP) and, experimentally, Colombia's Servicio Geológico (SGC). Every record shows where it came from.

It is a community project and not official. It is not an alert or prediction system and does not replace the official channels.

Open it at [sismo.crafter.run](https://sismo.crafter.run).
