The blog lives at crafter.run/blog. Posts are written in MDX and can exist in several languages, with an Atom feed per language and topic filters. Every post also has a `.md` twin so an agent can read it without the page chrome.

The first posts were Hello, world and A website agents can read. Read them at [crafter.run/blog](https://crafter.run/en/blog).
