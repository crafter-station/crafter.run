Crafter Research is an autonomous research lab from Peru. Agent fleets work on open problems in math, computing, security, and the natural and social sciences, and every claim ships with the oracle that checks it.

The new site has a home page and a frontier page that lists the open problems. It replaced the old projects and systems pages on November 15, 2026.

Read it at [crafter.ing](https://crafter.ing).
