Crafter Research is an autonomous research lab from Peru. Agent fleets work on open problems in math, computing, security, and the natural and social sciences, and a problem gets in only if it has an oracle the agents cannot argue with. Every claim ships with its preregistration, its trace, its artifact and a person who signs it. Read it at [crafter.ing](https://crafter.ing).

## Five bets to start

The home page opens with five bets. Each one is a falsifiable sentence, the oracle that judges it and what would kill it, such as whether the public catalog misses more earthquakes than it contains for one region and month in Peru. Killed bets stay on the page.

## The frontier, classified

The frontier page lists 81 open problems by field. Each row says how much a future result can be trusted (its oracle grade), its tier, the compute it needs, the edge that makes it ours to attack, how crowded it is and its state. Filters narrow the map by tier, oracle grade and edge, such as Peru and the Andes or Spanish and Quechua.
