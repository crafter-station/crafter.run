The Crew is for people who work on one area of Crafter alongside someone from the Core. You pick an area, work with the person who leads it and see after three months whether it fits.

The page lists the current Crew, and agents can read it through the new `list_crew` MCP tool. See it at [crafter.run/team/crew](https://crafter.run/en/team/crew).
