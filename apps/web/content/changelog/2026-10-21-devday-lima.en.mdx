DevDay Exchange Community: Lima was an independent community gathering on October 21, 2026, from 6pm to 9pm at the Faculty of Systems Engineering (FISI) of UNMSM. It recapped the public DevDay news, then spent most of the evening building with Codex. It was listed on the Codex Community Events calendar on [Luma](https://luma.com/1iz8daqt), which shows 86 people attended.

## Build together, no competition

The format was collaborative. Attendees worked on their own idea or a prepared exercise, alone or in small groups, with technical help in the room. Sharing progress was optional, and there were no required presentations.

## An entry flow for approved guests

Crafter Station built the event page on crafter.run with its own DevDay identity. Guests approved on Luma signed in with the same email, completed their entry details and could claim a personal Makeables badge.
