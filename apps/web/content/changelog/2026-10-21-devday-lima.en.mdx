DevDay Exchange Community: Lima was an independent community gathering on October 21, 2026, from 6pm to 9pm at the Faculty of Systems Engineering (FISI) of UNMSM. Three talks recapped the public DevDay news, followed by a live Codex demo. It was listed on the Codex Community Events calendar on [Luma](https://luma.com/1iz8daqt), where 86 guests were approved. The full story is in the [Journal recap](/en/blog/devday-exchange-lima-recap).

## A recap and a live demo

The evening opened with a DevDay recap in four parts, presented by Railly Hugo and Edward Ramos. For the demo, Codex built a raffle app for the event live from the terminal, and to close we prompted small games on makeables.dev/play. A build session was planned, but the opening ran long and there was no time left for it.

## An entry flow for approved guests

Crafter Station built the event page on crafter.run with its own DevDay identity. Guests approved on Luma signed in with the same email, completed their entry details and could claim a personal Makeables badge.
