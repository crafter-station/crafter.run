LatamBench reruns existing Latin American benchmarks (Trueque and CHOCLO) with a judge anchored to the reference answers. It publishes the transcripts and audits the benchmarks themselves.

It matters because nobody was comparing models on this independently. Results are reproducible, and the limits are declared on the site.

See the leaderboard at [latambench.org](https://latambench.org).
