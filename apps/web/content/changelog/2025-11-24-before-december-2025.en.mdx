This roundup collects the 9 smaller ships from the two years before December 2025, from Retest in December 2023 to scrapi in November 2025. All 9 came from Station. Most are tools for developers, such as an A/B testing platform, the first Crafter Station UI library and a way to turn websites into APIs. Language models run through several others, from a multi-LLM research pipeline to pong0, where models play tennis against each other. GitHunter, a GitHub exploration app with vector search, has the most stars of the group.

scrapi, the newest ship in this roundup, turns any website into a deterministic API. album0 lets you create a public photo album and share it with a link. ultra-deep-research runs a deep research pipeline across several language models.

## Everything that shipped

### 2023

- **04 Dec 2023**: [Retest](https://useretest.vercel.app): an A/B testing platform for quick product teams

### 2024

- **31 Aug 2024**: [krafter-ui](https://github.com/crafter-station/krafter-ui): the first Crafter Station UI library, published on npm

### 2025

- **06 Feb 2025**: [Gradual](https://trygradual.vercel.app/): a learning platform that breaks complex topics into small steps
- **01 May 2025**: [GitHunter](https://githunter.dev): "Hunt the git", a GitHub exploration app with vector search
- **07 Jun 2025**: [hipoteca-findr](https://hipoteca-ai.vercel.app): an AI tool to resolve mortgage uncertainty
- **19 Jul 2025**: [ultra-deep-research](https://ultra-deep-research.vercel.app): a multi-LLM deep research pipeline
- **25 Aug 2025**: [album0](https://album0.vercel.app): create and share public photo albums
- **14 Sep 2025**: [pong0](https://pong0.vercel.app/): LLMs play tennis against each other
- **24 Nov 2025**: [scrapi](https://scrapi.fast): turn any website into a deterministic API

## By the numbers

- 9 ships, all from Station
- 8 with a live site and 1 with a published package or release
- 47 GitHub stars in total (stars as of Nov 15, 2026)
