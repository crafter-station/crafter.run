Crafter Smash is a Super Smash Flash style fighter where the characters are members of the community, each with their own special moves. It supports one player against the CPU, online play, two players and a training mode.

It is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript on a Canvas, so you can read the whole game in one sitting.

Play at [smash.crafter.run](https://smash.crafter.run).
