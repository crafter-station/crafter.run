Crafter Smash is a free 2D platform fighter in the style of Super Smash Flash, and the fighters are members of the Crafter Station community drawn in pixel art. It runs in your browser on an HTML5 Canvas, with no dependencies and no build step. Play at [smash.crafter.run](https://smash.crafter.run).

<Clip src="/changelog/crafter-smash/fight.mp4" poster="/changelog/crafter-smash/fight.webp" size="1280x800" alt="Railly fighting Jibaru on the Templo Crafter stage, landing hits and specials while the damage percentage climbs" caption="Damage builds as a percentage, and knockback grows with it." />

## A real fighting game

Damage and knockback follow the Melee formula, and the game includes hitlag, DI, techs, wavedashing, shields that break, grabs and ledge play. Each fighter has their own specials, a super move and a taunt.

## Four ways to play

Fight the CPU on levels 1 to 9, play online, play two players on one keyboard, or practice in training mode. Controllers work through the Gamepad API, and keys can be remapped from the controls menu.

## Read the whole game

The game is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, so you can read it end to end. Physics, characters, the CPU and stages each live in their own file.

## Try it

Play at [smash.crafter.run](https://smash.crafter.run). To run it locally, clone the [repository](https://github.com/crafter-games/crafter-smash) and open the page in your browser.

```sh
git clone https://github.com/crafter-games/crafter-smash
open crafter-smash/index.html
```
