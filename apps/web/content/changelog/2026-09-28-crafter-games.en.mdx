Crafter Games is the home of the games the community builds, from fighting and artillery games to ones that teach. Each entry has a poster and a short loop so you can see it before you play.

The site is open source, and adding a game is one entry in a list.

Browse the catalog at [crafter.games](https://crafter.games).
