November 2026 had 4 smaller ships up to November 15, all from Station. Two explain a subject you can explore, GIZA with a 3D tour of the pyramids of Giza and La universidad en cifras with an interactive version of the Minedu report on higher education. The other two are tools, ig-cli for Instagram creator research and twin for syncing Claude Code between your Macs.
