Craft Ones is an original turn-based artillery game made in Peru. Play hot-seat on one screen, or open a room and send the link to a friend. It runs on the web, as a Discord Activity, natively on macOS and on iPhone.

It is built with dotframe, the engine from the same org.

Play at [craft-ones.crafter.run](https://craft-ones.crafter.run).
