Craft Ones is a free, original 1v1 artillery game made in Peru. Pick a critter, a coat and a map, then take turns aiming, charging and firing on one screen or online with a link. There is no sign-up. Play at [craft-ones.crafter.run](https://craft-ones.crafter.run).

## Pick your critter and your map

Each player chooses a critter and a coat, and every critter has its own power in slot 7. Maps range from hanging gardens to sandstone bridges, and the terrain breaks where shots land.

<Figure src="/changelog/craft-ones/setup.webp" alt="Craft Ones setup screen with Player 1 and Player 2 critters, the Cloudbreak Valley map and buttons to start a duel or play online" size="1280x800" caption="Start a duel on one screen, or play online with a link." />

## Fifteen seconds a turn

Each critter starts with 100 HP and gets 15 seconds per turn to move, pick one of six tools and shoot. With the slingshot controls you press on the field, pull back and let go, and the length of the pull is the power. Wind changes every turn.

<Figure src="/changelog/craft-ones/arena.webp" alt="A Craft Ones turn with the llama aiming, the wind indicator, the tool bar and the power meter" size="1280x800" caption="The HUD shows the timer, the wind, your tools and the shot power." />

## Play anywhere

Craft Ones runs on the web, as a Discord Activity, natively on macOS and on iPhone. It is built with dotframe, the game engine from the same organization.

## Try it

Open [craft-ones.crafter.run](https://craft-ones.crafter.run) and click Play, or install it on Discord from the same page. The source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/crafter-games/craft-ones).
