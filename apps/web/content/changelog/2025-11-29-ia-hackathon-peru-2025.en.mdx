IA Hackathon Peru 2025 was a 24-hour, in-person AI hackathon on November 29 and 30, 2025, at UPCH La Molina in Lima. It was presented by The Hackathon Company with Crafter Station among its partners, and the event site stays online as its record at [peru.ai-hackathon.co](https://peru.ai-hackathon.co).

<Figure src="/changelog/ia-hackathon-peru-2025/hero.webp" alt="IA Hackathon Peru 2025 site header with the partners and the November 29 to 30 dates" size="1280x800" caption="The event site after the hackathon, with the recap, workshops and gallery." />

## An open format

There was no fixed problem to solve. Participants signed up individually and formed teams of up to five during the event. Teams handed in their repository and slides, pitched to mentors in a closed round, and five finalists pitched to the judges and everyone in the room. Projects were scored on innovation and impact, technical execution, viability, and pitch and UX.

## Workshops before the weekend

From November 18 to 28, free online workshops prepared participants for the hackathon. The site keeps every session with its recording.

## The record

The site lists the judging panel, the agenda for both days, the participants and the submitted projects, and links to a recap video and photo gallery. According to UPC, first place went to a team that included two of its Software Engineering students.
