The smaller ships of the period, each with a live site, a published package or a release.

- [Astra](https://astra.crafter.run): a WebGPU star field built with vgpu
- [Kliq](https://kliq.crafter.run): mechanical keyboard sounds for your Mac that go quiet during calls
- [v-prism](https://v-prism.crafter.run): a glass prism splitting light into a rainbow on WebGPU
- [Crafter Knot](https://crafter-knot.vercel.app): the Crafter Station mark as 3D tubes on WebGPU
- [Crafter Glass](https://crafter-glass.crafter.run): the Crafter Station mark in glass on WebGPU
- [WhatsApp Components](https://whatsapp-components.crafter.run): a WhatsApp chat in React that passes for the real thing
- [Makeables](https://makeables.dev): stickers, badges and cards designed with your coding agent, with a Wallet companion
- [uber-cli](https://github.com/crafter-station/uber-cli): an agent-first CLI and MCP server for Uber fares, rides and receipts
- [Lava](https://lava-wgsl.vercel.app): pahoehoe lava rendered live on WebGPU in WGSL
