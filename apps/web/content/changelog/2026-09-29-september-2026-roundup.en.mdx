September 2026 had 9 smaller ships, all from Station, and 5 of them render on WebGPU, among them Astra, a star field built with vgpu, and Lava, which renders pahoehoe lava live in WGSL. Kliq brings mechanical keyboard sounds to the Mac. The rest include a WhatsApp chat in React, Makeables for designing stickers and cards with your coding agent, and uber-cli for Uber fares and rides.

Kliq, the most starred ship of the month, plays mechanical keyboard sounds on your Mac and goes quiet during calls. Astra renders a star field on WebGPU with vgpu. Crafter Knot draws the Crafter Station mark as 3D tubes on WebGPU.

## Everything that shipped

### Week of Sep 1

- **06 Sep**: [Astra](https://astra.crafter.run): a WebGPU star field built with vgpu

### Week of Sep 7

- **13 Sep**: [Kliq](https://kliq.crafter.run): mechanical keyboard sounds for your Mac that go quiet during calls

### Week of Sep 21

- **21 Sep**: [v-prism](https://v-prism.crafter.run): a glass prism splitting light into a rainbow on WebGPU
- **24 Sep**: [Crafter Knot](https://crafter-knot.vercel.app): the Crafter Station mark as 3D tubes on WebGPU
- **24 Sep**: [Crafter Glass](https://crafter-glass.crafter.run): the Crafter Station mark in glass on WebGPU
- **25 Sep**: [WhatsApp Components](https://whatsapp-components.crafter.run): a WhatsApp chat in React that passes for the real thing

### Week of Sep 28

- **28 Sep**: [Makeables](https://makeables.dev): stickers, badges and cards designed with your coding agent, with a Wallet companion
- **29 Sep**: [uber-cli](https://github.com/crafter-station/uber-cli): an agent-first CLI and MCP server for Uber fares, rides and receipts
- **29 Sep**: [Lava](https://lava-wgsl.vercel.app): pahoehoe lava rendered live on WebGPU in WGSL

## By the numbers

- 9 ships, all from Station
- 8 with a live site and 3 with a published package or release (2 have both)
- 48 GitHub stars in total (stars as of Nov 15, 2026)
