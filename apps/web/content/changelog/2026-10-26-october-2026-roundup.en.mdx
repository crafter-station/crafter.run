October 2026 had 4 smaller ships, and it is the first roundup in this series with work from Games. Games shipped two of them, Column Depot, a 3D warehouse game that teaches how ClickHouse stores and reads data, and THE ONES, a first-person horror game for Windows. Station shipped posture, which sends slouch alerts from your AirPods, and Crafter Clawd, a wearable cardboard costume kit with cut files.

posture, the most starred ship of the month, reads your AirPods to warn you when you slouch, from a macOS menu bar app or a CLI. Column Depot teaches how ClickHouse stores and reads data by letting you run a 3D warehouse. It is unofficial.

## Everything that shipped

- **02 Oct**: [Column Depot](https://learnclickhouse.crafter.run): a 3D warehouse game that teaches how ClickHouse stores and reads data (unofficial)
- **06 Oct**: [THE ONES](https://theones.crafter.games): a first-person horror game set in rural Japan in 1998, for Windows
- **26 Oct**: [posture](https://posture.crafter.run): slouch alerts from your AirPods in a macOS menu bar app and CLI
- **26 Oct**: [Crafter Clawd](https://clawd.crafter.run): a wearable cardboard Clawd costume kit with cut files

## By the numbers

- 4 ships, 2 from Games and 2 from Station
- 4 with a live site and 2 with a published package or release (2 have both)
- 15 GitHub stars in total (stars as of Nov 15, 2026)
