The smaller ships of the period, each with a live site, a published package or a release.

- [Column Depot](https://learnclickhouse.crafter.run): a 3D warehouse game that teaches how ClickHouse stores and reads data (unofficial)
- [THE ONES](https://theones.crafter.games): a first-person horror game set in rural Japan in 1998, for Windows
- [posture](https://posture.crafter.run): slouch alerts from your AirPods in a macOS menu bar app and CLI
- [Crafter Clawd](https://clawd.crafter.run): a wearable cardboard Clawd costume kit with cut files
