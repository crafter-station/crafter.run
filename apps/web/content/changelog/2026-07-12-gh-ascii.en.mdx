gh-ascii converts your avatar into ASCII art and pulls your stats (languages, repos, stars, commits) from the GitHub API. It needs no hosting and no API key, and the repo ships a GitHub Actions workflow that refreshes the card daily.

Generate yours at [gh.crafter.run](https://gh.crafter.run).
