Land vs People draws every country and continent as about 1,000 blocks, then moves the same blocks to show where people live and where the money is. A map by area makes empty places look important, and this one lets you watch the weight shift. It covers 213 places. Open it at [land-vs-people.crafter.ing](https://land-vs-people.crafter.ing).

## Peru, three ways

Each block on the Peru map is 0.1% of the country. By land, Lima Metropolitana holds 0.20%. By people it holds 32%, and by GDP 47%. Loreto goes the other way, from 29% of the land to 3.4% of the people and 1.6% of the GDP.

<Figure src="/changelog/land-vs-people/gdp.webp" alt="Land vs People map of Peru by GDP, with Lima Metropolitana taking most of the blocks" size="1280x800" caption="Peru by GDP. Lima Metropolitana holds 47%." />

## Every place has its numbers

Below the map, each country page lists its regions with their share of land, people and GDP. It also explains how the map is built. Regions sit on an equal-area grid, each one gets a whole number of blocks in proportion to its share, and every block is matched to one block on the next map so neighbors stay together.

## Try it

Open [land-vs-people.crafter.ing](https://land-vs-people.crafter.ing), press 1, 2 or 3 to switch the measure and space to pause. Press `/` to search for a place, or go straight to one by its code, such as [/per](https://land-vs-people.crafter.ing/per) for Peru.
