Build4Venezuela was an online humanitarian hackathon to build tech for Venezuela after the earthquakes. The site counts more than 1,000 builders and more than 80 projects. Crafter Station is one of the partners listed on [build4venezuela.com](https://build4venezuela.com), next to Moraleja Studio, The Hackathon Company and Nucleo, and the site's code lives in the crafter-station GitHub organization.

## The build showcase

The closing event on July 1, 2026 at 6pm UTC-5 presented what teams built during the hackathon, such as people search, shelters, donations, verified information and citizen reports.

## Built for low signal and real need

The site asked builders to pick a concrete problem, check what already exists and build for someone in Venezuela with low signal and low battery. It listed the kinds of tools that help most.

<Figure src="/changelog/build4venezuela/ideas.webp" alt="Build4Venezuela project ideas, from verified shelter directories to WhatsApp bots and aid tracking" size="1280x800" caption="Six kinds of tools the site asked builders to focus on." />

## The work continues

After the closing, Build4Venezuela moved from a sprint into a longer collective effort. Builders can keep improving projects, join an existing team or propose a new problem.
