Build4Venezuela was an online humanitarian hackathon to build tech solutions for Venezuela. The closing event on July 1, 2026 presented what was built, such as people search, shelters, donations, verified information and citizen reports.

The site lists the projects, builders and open needs, and the work continues in the open at [build4venezuela.com](https://build4venezuela.com).
