Ships is the community's shelf of what its members build. The platform arrived on August 11, 2026 together with the `@crafter/cli` package and agent-driven onboarding, so an agent can draft your profile and your first Ship. Published Ships can carry updates and media, and visitors can upvote them.

The list is also available through the public API at api.crafter.run. Browse it at [crafter.run/ships](https://crafter.run/en/ships).
