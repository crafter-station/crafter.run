Every skill you install takes room in your agent's context window. skillkit scans your session files locally, counts invocations and shows what to prune. It works with Claude Code, Cursor, Codex and more, and nothing leaves your machine.

Run `bunx @crafter/skillkit stats` or read the docs at [skillkit.crafter.run](https://skillkit.crafter.run).
