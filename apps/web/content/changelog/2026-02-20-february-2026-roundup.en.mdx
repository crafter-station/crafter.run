The smaller ships of the period, each with a live site, a published package or a release.

- [EasyTalk](https://easy-talk-five.vercel.app): real-time multilingual chat where everyone reads their own language
- [PlatoSano](https://food-peru.vercel.app): plan family meals with healthy Peruvian recipes from the national health institute
- [@crafter/mermaid](https://github.com/crafter-station/mermaid): a lightweight, zero-dependency Mermaid renderer
- [C3.chat](https://c3.crafter.run/): a chat app by Crafter Station
- [Espik](https://espik.crafter.run): real-time speech translation
- [pushup.ai](https://pushup.crafter.run/): count push-ups with computer vision and pose estimation
- [SkillCraft](https://skillcraft-rouge.vercel.app): generate SKILL.md files from any documentation URL
- [Visual Reference](https://visual-reference.vercel.app): browse, compare and copy design tokens from real sites
- [Speech to Jira](https://s2jira.crafter.run/): speak your tasks and watch a kanban board build itself
