February 2026 had 9 smaller ships, all from Station. Language and voice show up most, with EasyTalk's multilingual chat, Espik's real-time speech translation and Speech to Jira, which builds a kanban board from what you say. Developers and agents got SkillCraft, which writes SKILL.md files from documentation, and a zero-dependency Mermaid renderer. Two projects are about health, PlatoSano for healthy Peruvian recipes and pushup.ai for counting push-ups.

C3.chat, a chat app by Crafter Station, has the most stars of the month. Espik translates speech in real time. pushup.ai counts your push-ups with computer vision and pose estimation.

## Everything that shipped

### Week of Feb 1

- **01 Feb**: [EasyTalk](https://easy-talk-five.vercel.app): real-time multilingual chat where everyone reads their own language
- **01 Feb**: [PlatoSano](https://food-peru.vercel.app): plan family meals with healthy Peruvian recipes from the national health institute

### Week of Feb 2

- **06 Feb**: [@crafter/mermaid](https://github.com/crafter-station/mermaid): a lightweight, zero-dependency Mermaid renderer
- **07 Feb**: [C3.chat](https://c3.crafter.run/): a chat app by Crafter Station

### Week of Feb 9

- **12 Feb**: [Espik](https://espik.crafter.run): real-time speech translation

### Week of Feb 16

- **17 Feb**: [pushup.ai](https://pushup.crafter.run/): count push-ups with computer vision and pose estimation
- **17 Feb**: [SkillCraft](https://skillcraft-rouge.vercel.app): generate SKILL.md files from any documentation URL
- **18 Feb**: [Visual Reference](https://visual-reference.vercel.app): browse, compare and copy design tokens from real sites
- **20 Feb**: [Speech to Jira](https://s2jira.crafter.run/): speak your tasks and watch a kanban board build itself

## By the numbers

- 9 ships, all from Station
- 8 with a live site and 1 with a published package or release
- 31 GitHub stars in total (stars as of Nov 15, 2026)
