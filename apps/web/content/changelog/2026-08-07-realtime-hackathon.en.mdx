The realtime hackathon was an online weekend for building live, multiplayer or agentic AI products with Portal. The event page credits it to Portal and Crafter Station, and it ran from August 7 to 9, 2026. The format and schedule are still at [hack.useportal.co](https://hack.useportal.co).

<Figure src="/changelog/realtime-hackathon/intro.webp" alt="Opening screen of the realtime hackathon page, a wireframe hand with the Portal and Crafter Station credit and a start button" size="1280x800" caption="The event page opens with a start button and a scroll-driven ride." />

## Five tracks

Teams picked one of five tracks, or none. Multiplayer covered shared live rooms, Live streaming broadcast state to a crowd, Real-time location built living maps, AI agents acted on live signals, and Wild signal took the experiments that fit nowhere else.

<Clip src="/changelog/realtime-hackathon/portal.mp4" poster="/changelog/realtime-hackathon/portal.webp" size="1280x800" alt="Scrolling the realtime hackathon page from the tracks intro into the Multiplayer track" caption="Each track gets its own scene as you scroll." />

## 39 hours, online

Teams of one to four people had from Friday at 7pm to Sunday at 10am (UTC-5) to ship a working product built with Portal. Registration was free, mentors held office hours in Discord on Saturday, and the top five projects were shown on Discord on Sunday evening.

## See the event

The tracks, schedule and FAQ are on [hack.useportal.co](https://hack.useportal.co), and the event is listed with the other Crafter hackathons at [crafter.run/hackathons](https://crafter.run/en/hackathons).
