January 2026 had 13 smaller ships, all from Station, the most of any roundup in this series. Several are tools for working with AI, from ParseBench, which compares document parsing providers, to Crafter Code and Cadence, which track and test AI agents. Others are lighter, like karaoke0 with word-by-word lyrics or a vintage photo booth app built with Tauri. The month also brought The Shipping Bible, the Crafter Station shipping playbook for LatAm builders.

i18n, an internationalization experiment at i18n.crafter.run, has the most stars of the month. latex0 is an open source LaTeX editor with AI built in. ParseBench runs the same document through several parsing providers and shows the results side by side.

## Everything that shipped

### Week of Jan 1

- **01 Jan**: [Agentic Vision Board](https://agenticboard.xyz): make your 2026 vision board with AI-generated images
- **01 Jan**: [Let's Tag Fast](https://www.letstag.fast/): a Chrome extension to tag many LinkedIn users in one post
- **03 Jan**: [The Shipping Bible](https://theshippingbible.com): the Crafter Station shipping playbook for LatAm builders

### Week of Jan 12

- **12 Jan**: [@crafter/flow](https://github.com/crafter-station/flow): a zero-dependency infinite canvas library for React
- **12 Jan**: [cal.date](https://cal.date): a dating platform built on direct calendar booking
- **14 Jan**: [photo-booth](https://github.com/crafter-station/photo-booth): a vintage photo booth desktop app built with Tauri, React and Rust
- **14 Jan**: [Claude en pañales](https://claude-meme.vercel.app): share your Claude team

### Week of Jan 19

- **21 Jan**: [ParseBench](https://parse.crafter.run/): compare document parsing providers side by side
- **24 Jan**: [i18n](https://i18n.crafter.run/): an internationalization experiment at i18n.crafter.run
- **25 Jan**: [Crafter Code](https://crafter-code.vercel.app): an agent-first IDE that tracks several AI agent sessions at once

### Week of Jan 26

- **27 Jan**: [karaoke0](https://karaoke0.crafter.run): a lyrics player with word-by-word highlighting
- **28 Jan**: [latex0](https://latex0.vercel.app): an AI-powered LaTeX editor, open source
- **31 Jan**: [Cadence](https://cadence.crafter.run): large-scale parallel testing and optimization for AI agents

## By the numbers

- 13 ships, all from Station
- 11 with a live site and 2 with a published package or release
- 87 GitHub stars in total (stars as of Nov 15, 2026)
