The smaller ships of the period, each with a live site, a published package or a release.

- [Agentic Vision Board](https://agenticboard.xyz): make your 2026 vision board with AI-generated images
- [Let's Tag Fast](https://www.letstag.fast/): a Chrome extension to tag many LinkedIn users in one post
- [The Shipping Bible](https://theshippingbible.com): the Crafter Station shipping playbook for LatAm builders
- [@crafter/flow](https://github.com/crafter-station/flow): a zero-dependency infinite canvas library for React
- [cal.date](https://cal.date): a dating platform built on direct calendar booking
- [photo-booth](https://github.com/crafter-station/photo-booth): a vintage photo booth desktop app built with Tauri, React and Rust
- [Claude en pañales](https://claude-meme.vercel.app): share your Claude team
- [ParseBench](https://parse.crafter.run/): compare document parsing providers side by side
- [i18n](https://i18n.crafter.run/): an internationalization experiment at i18n.crafter.run
- [Crafter Code](https://crafter-code.vercel.app): an agent-first IDE that tracks several AI agent sessions at once
- [karaoke0](https://karaoke0.crafter.run): a lyrics player with word-by-word highlighting
- [latex0](https://latex0.vercel.app): an AI-powered LaTeX editor, open source
- [Cadence](https://cadence.crafter.run): large-scale parallel testing and optimization for AI agents
