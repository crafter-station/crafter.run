Taste hacking is our live design engineering course with AI agents, taught in Spanish. It now lives at [tastehacking.dev](https://tastehacking.dev), where you learn to take apart any site, animation or video and name it so your agent can build it.

## A public guide to design engineering

[/design-engineering](https://tastehacking.dev/design-engineering) covers what a design engineer does, the 14 roles that exist today and the core concepts, each with its source. It also has guides for doing design work with agents: DESIGN.md, Claude Code and Codex.

## Words to ask an agent with

The [vocabulary](https://tastehacking.dev/vocabulario) collects 65 terms in three layers: style, time and code. Each term has a short definition, and some play live, like tween against spring.

<Figure src="/changelog/taste-hacking/vocabulario.webp" alt="Taste hacking vocabulary with search and the Style layer terms" size="1280x800" caption="Search for a term or browse a whole layer." />

## Breakdowns in the open

Eleven pieces from the course are public: Apple's liquid glass, Lando Norris's stack, Nubank's brand system, a Nothing product film and more. Each one shows the before, the after and the lesson.

## Try it

Open [tastehacking.dev](https://tastehacking.dev), read the guide and the vocabulary without an account, and leave your email for edition 02. If you took edition 01, sign in to see the material and leave a testimonial.

## What changed

- Taste hacking moves from hack0.dev to its own domain, tastehacking.dev.
- /taste-hacking on hack0.dev redirects to tastehacking.dev ([#204](https://github.com/crafter-station/hack0/pull/204)).
- Testimonials and their review move with the course ([#205](https://github.com/crafter-station/hack0/pull/205)).
