The smaller ships of the period, each with a live site, a published package or a release.

- [@crafter/rn-ai-elements](https://github.com/crafter-station/rn-ai-elements): a React Native port of Vercel's ai-elements
- [@crafter/charts](https://charts.crafter.run): terminal-native charts, from sparklines to candlesticks
- [surfacer](https://surfacer.dev): map a surface once and emit CLIs, agent tools and native binaries from one IR
- [Motif](https://motif.crafter.run): carve scannable barcodes through the silhouette of any shape
- [Timeline Peru](https://timeline-peru.vercel.app): an interactive timeline of Peruvian history
- [Screen2Prompt](https://screen2prompt.supalab.dev/): turn screen feedback into prompts for AI coding tools
- [@crafter/neon-cli](https://github.com/crafter-station/neon-cli): an agent-first CLI for Neon Postgres usage, billing and branches
- [doomain](https://github.com/crafter-station/doomain): link your Vercel project and domain in seconds
- [survey-cli](https://survey-cli.crafter.run): type-safe surveys you run from the terminal
