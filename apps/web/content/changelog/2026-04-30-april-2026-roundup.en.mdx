April 2026 had 9 smaller ships, 8 from Station and 1 from Research. Most are developer tools, and several are built for the terminal or for agents, such as @crafter/charts, survey-cli, neon-cli and surfacer, which emits CLIs and agent tools from one IR. doomain, the most starred ship of the month, links a Vercel project and a domain in seconds. Research added Timeline Peru, an interactive timeline of Peruvian history.

surfacer maps a surface once and emits CLIs, agent tools and native binaries from one IR. @crafter/charts draws charts in the terminal, from sparklines to candlesticks. Motif carves a scannable barcode through the silhouette of any shape you give it.

## Everything that shipped

### Week of Apr 6

- **09 Apr**: [@crafter/rn-ai-elements](https://github.com/crafter-station/rn-ai-elements): a React Native port of Vercel's ai-elements
- **10 Apr**: [@crafter/charts](https://charts.crafter.run): terminal-native charts, from sparklines to candlesticks

### Week of Apr 13

- **14 Apr**: [surfacer](https://surfacer.dev): map a surface once and emit CLIs, agent tools and native binaries from one IR
- **15 Apr**: [Motif](https://motif.crafter.run): carve scannable barcodes through the silhouette of any shape

### Week of Apr 20

- **20 Apr**: [Timeline Peru](https://timeline-peru.vercel.app): an interactive timeline of Peruvian history
- **20 Apr**: [Screen2Prompt](https://screen2prompt.supalab.dev/): turn screen feedback into prompts for AI coding tools
- **23 Apr**: [@crafter/neon-cli](https://github.com/crafter-station/neon-cli): an agent-first CLI for Neon Postgres usage, billing and branches

### Week of Apr 27

- **28 Apr**: [doomain](https://github.com/crafter-station/doomain): link your Vercel project and domain in seconds
- **30 Apr**: [survey-cli](https://survey-cli.crafter.run): type-safe surveys you run from the terminal
