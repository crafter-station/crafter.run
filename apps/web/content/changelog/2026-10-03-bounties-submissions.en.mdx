Bounties are small community challenges with a deadline: build something, share it and put your work in the running. Since October 3, 2026 each bounty has its own page at `/bounties/[id]` with the brief, the deadline and a form to submit your work. See the board at [crafter.run/bounties](https://crafter.run/en/bounties).

<Figure src="/changelog/bounties-submissions/board.webp" alt="The Crafter Station bounties page with the headline Bounties and buttons to explore the board or read how it works" size="1280x800" caption="The board lists every bounty, open or closed." />

## Submit from the bounty page

Each bounty page holds the challenge, the steps and the deadline in Lima time. You send your entry from the same page, signing in with WhatsApp or any social account.

## Winners on the board

Once a bounty closes, its page shows the winners with a link to each post and project. Bounty 01 asked people to build something with AI over a weekend and post a video about it, and its three winning projects are now listed.

<Figure src="/changelog/bounties-submissions/winners.webp" alt="Bounty 01 winners on crafter.run: PixelForge, Luca and Fact-checker, each with links to the post and the project" size="1280x800" caption="Three projects built with AI over a weekend." />

## Try it

Open [crafter.run/bounties](https://crafter.run/en/bounties) to read the last challenge and its winners, and join the community to hear when the next one opens.
