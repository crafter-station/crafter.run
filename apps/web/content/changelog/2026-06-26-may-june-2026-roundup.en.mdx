May and June 2026 add up to 5 smaller ships, all from Station, so this roundup covers both months. Three work with voice and audio. Luvy saves voice notes for race day, s1ng turns a YouTube link into karaoke and Maca types what you say anywhere. The other two are about places and work. Reporte VE is a citizen map of outages in Venezuela, and Radar de empleos builds LinkedIn job searches by role and country.

Reporte VE, the most starred ship of these two months, maps power, water and supply outages in Venezuela and is private by design. Luvy saves voice notes for race day that unlock when the race starts. Radar de empleos generates LinkedIn job and post searches for a role and a country.

## Everything that shipped

- **18 May**: [Luvy](https://luvy.run): voice notes for race day that unlock when the race starts
- **02 Jun**: [s1ng](https://s1ng.crafter.run/): paste a YouTube link and get karaoke, with romaji for Japanese and Korean
- **10 Jun**: [Maca](https://github.com/crafter-station/maca-releases): hold Fn, speak, and your words are typed anywhere; this is its release channel
- **25 Jun**: [Radar de empleos](https://in.crafter.run): generate LinkedIn job and post searches by role and country
- **26 Jun**: [Reporte VE](https://ve.crafter.run/): a citizen map of power, water and supply outages in Venezuela, private by design
