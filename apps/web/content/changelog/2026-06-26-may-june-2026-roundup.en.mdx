The smaller ships of the period, each with a live site, a published package or a release.

- [Luvy](https://luvy.run): voice notes for race day that unlock when the race starts
- [s1ng](https://s1ng.crafter.run/): paste a YouTube link and get karaoke, with romaji for Japanese and Korean
- [Maca](https://github.com/crafter-station/maca-releases): hold Fn, speak, and your words are typed anywhere; this is its release channel
- [Radar de empleos](https://in.crafter.run): generate LinkedIn job and post searches by role and country
- [Reporte VE](https://ve.crafter.run/): a citizen map of power, water and supply outages in Venezuela, private by design
