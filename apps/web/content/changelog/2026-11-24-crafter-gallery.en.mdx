Crafter Gallery puts the photos from every Crafter Station event in one place. It starts with 38 photos from two events, Hot Reload 01 and DevDay Exchange Community: Lima. Open it at [crafter.run/events/gallery](https://crafter.run/en/events/gallery).

<Clip src="/changelog/crafter-gallery/demo.mp4" poster="/changelog/crafter-gallery/demo.webp" size="1280x800" alt="Filtering the gallery to Hot Reload, opening a photo in the lightbox, moving to the next two with the arrow keys and closing it" caption="Filter by event, then move through the photos with the arrow keys." />

## One album per event

Each event has its own album with its cover, date, city and photo count. A stamp in the corner of every photo shows which event it belongs to, and the chips above the grid filter it to a single event. The filter stays in the URL, so a link to `?event=hot-reload` opens with only those photos.

<Figure src="/changelog/crafter-gallery/albums.webp" alt="Gallery page with two album cards, DevDay Exchange Community: Lima with 22 photos and Hot Reload 01 with 16 photos" size="1280x800" caption="Albums link to each event's own gallery." />

## Full HD, with a soft start

Every photo opens in a lightbox at 1920 pixels and has its own link. A tiny blurred preview loads first and the photo fades in once it is ready, so a slow connection shows the right picture instead of an empty box. The neighbouring photos load in the background while you look at one.

<Figure src="/changelog/crafter-gallery/grid.webp" alt="Gallery grid with event chips for All, Hot Reload and DevDay Exchange, and photos marked with a DevDay stamp" size="1280x800" caption="Each stamp tells you which event a photo is from." />

## Each event keeps its look

The event galleries keep the identity of their event. Hot Reload photos carry a warm grade with grain, in the colors of its brand. DevDay photos use a cool, muted grade that matches the OpenAI DevDay style. Both recap posts link to their gallery.

## On the home page

The Events section on the home page now shows the latest photos in a grid that links to the full gallery, and the events agenda lists every album.

<Figure src="/changelog/crafter-gallery/home.webp" alt="Home page Events section with a photo grid and a link to view the full gallery of 38 photos" size="1280x800" caption="The home page grid opens the gallery." />

## Try it

Open [crafter.run/events/gallery](https://crafter.run/en/events/gallery), or jump to [Hot Reload 01](https://crafter.run/en/events/hot-reload/1/gallery) or [DevDay Exchange Lima](https://crafter.run/en/events/devday-lima/gallery).

## What changed

- New gallery at `/events/gallery`, with album cards, event filters and a Full HD lightbox.
- New galleries for Hot Reload 01 and DevDay Exchange Lima, each in its event's theme.
- A photo grid in the home page Events section and an albums row in the events agenda.
- The Hot Reload 01 and DevDay Exchange Lima recap posts link to their galleries.
