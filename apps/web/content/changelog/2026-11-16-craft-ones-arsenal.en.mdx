Craft Ones is now played over several lives: each knockout drops you back onto the same map, craters and all, and the first to land three knockouts wins. Five new weapons and a row of rules in setup come with it. Play at [craft-ones.crafter.run](https://craft-ones.crafter.run).

## Three lives on one map

Each player starts with 3 lives, or 1 or 5 if you pick them in setup. When you get knocked out you come back at 100 health on the solid ground closest to your spawn, away from your rival, and the terrain stays as it was. Falling into the void costs a life too.

<Clip src="/changelog/craft-ones-arsenal/ko.mp4" poster="/changelog/craft-ones-arsenal/ko.webp" size="1280x800" alt="The guinea pig fires the huaca drill over the arch, the llama drops to 0 health and comes back at 100 as the notice P2 knocked out, 2 lives left appears" caption="The llama loses a life and comes back at 100 on the same map." />

## Five new weapons

The hotbar grows from six tools to eleven. The choclo bursts into five popcorn kernels, the condor bombs from above, the huaca drill bores into rock and blows up inside it, the llama spit barely hurts but shoves hard, and the totora bridge builds ground where it lands. Weapons sit on 1 through 0 and the minus key, and C is still your critter's power.

<Clip src="/changelog/craft-ones-arsenal/bore.mp4" poster="/changelog/craft-ones-arsenal/bore.webp" size="1280x800" alt="The huaca drill enters the stone arch, digs a tunnel and blows up inside, leaving a large crater" caption="The huaca drill digs into the rock and blows up inside it." />

## Rules that combine

Setup has a new row of rules. Tide raises the water from round 8 and drowns whoever is below it. Roulette hands you a random weapon each round, the same one for both players. Moon cuts gravity to less than half, and Big Boom makes every blast 60% bigger. Online, the host picks the rules and the guest sees them change live.

## Watch the match again

When a match ends, the REPLAY button or the V key plays it back exactly as it happened. Matches stay saved while the page is open, setup lists them under REPLAYS, and holding down plays them at 4x.

<Figure src="/changelog/craft-ones-arsenal/replay.webp" alt="A Craft Ones match replay with the Replay 1/1 bar and the red replay tag" size="1280x800" caption="Q and E switch between saved matches." />

## Try it

Open [craft-ones.crafter.run](https://craft-ones.crafter.run), pick the rules in setup, and play on one screen or share the link to play online.
